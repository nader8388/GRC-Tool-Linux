Important Notice — Please Read Before Proceeding
Data Handling & Storage
All assessment data entered into this platform — including control findings, notes, evidence references, and file attachments — is stored locally on this machine in a SQLite database. You are responsible for ensuring this device is secured appropriately and that access is restricted to authorized personnel only. Do not leave assessment sessions unattended on shared or unsecured machines.
File Attachments & Sensitive Data
This platform allows you to attach files as evidence. You are solely responsible for any files you choose to upload or attach. Do not attach files containing classified, export-controlled, or otherwise restricted information unless you have verified that this system is authorized to process such data. Attachments are stored locally alongside the database.
AI Assessment Feature
The AI Assessment feature sends your testing notes and control descriptions to Anthropic's API for analysis. Do not include sensitive, classified, or personally identifiable information in AI assessment inputs. AI-generated recommendations are advisory only — they are not authoritative compliance determinations. All AI outputs must be independently reviewed and verified by a qualified assessor before being used in any formal compliance report or submission. The platform and its AI feature do not constitute a compliance certification of any kind.
